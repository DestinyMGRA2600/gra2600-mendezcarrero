How to make Cinnamon Rolls

Home   Dough and Filling   Prepare the Cinnamon Roll   How to bake

Bake the Cinnamon Rolls
After the dough has been rolled, place the rolls upright in a greased 9x13 inch baking pan. Cover the pan with a towel and let the rolls rise for an extra 30 minutes. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit while the rolls are rising. After the rolls have risen, place them in the oven and bake for 25-30 minutes or until they are golden brown. Once the rolls are done baking, remove them from the oven and let them cool for a few minutes before serving.
Add the Cream Cheese Frosting on top
In a bowl add 1 stick of butter and 1 package of cream cheese. Pour a drop of vanilla extract, add 2 cups of powdered sugar and mix until well combined. With a spatula, spread the frosting evenly over all the cinnamon rolls.

Serve the cinnamon rolls warm and enjoy!
