How to make Cinnamon Rolls

Home   Dough and Filling   Prepare the Cinnamon Roll   How to bake

Bake
Add the cinnamon rolls to a greased 9x13 inch baking pan, add a towel on top and let them rise for 30 minutes. After 30 minutes remove the towel and preheat the oven to 350 degrees Ferinheit and bake the rolls for 25-30 minutes or until they are golden brown. After the rolls have baked, remove them from the oven and let them cool for 10 minutes before adding the cream cheese frosting on top.
Make the Cream Cheese frosting
In a small bowl add 1 stick of butter and 1 package of cream cheese, add 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract and add 2 cups of powdered sugar. Mix until well combined. With a spatula spread the frosting evenly over the cooled cinnamon rolls.

Enjoy!
