How to make Cinnamon Rolls

Home   Dough and Filling   Prepare the Cinnamon Roll   How to bake

The dough
Heat up 2 cups of warm milk and add a drop of vanilla to it. Pour it into a large bowl to warm milk. Add two packets of dry yeast onto the milk and mix it. Wait until this mixture bubbles up to add the rest of the ingredients. Add a 2 1/3 cup of melted butter, 4 eggs, 2 teaspoons of salt, 2 1/2 cups of granulated sugar and 8 cups of flour. Mix all the ingredients together until it forms a dough. Remove the dough from the bowl and Knead the dough on a flat surface for 5 minutes or more. The consistency of the dough should be soft, manageable and stretchy. If the dough is sticky continue to knead it. After you finish kneading the dough, oil the bowl and place the dough back into the bowl. Cover the bowl with a towel and let it rise for 1 hour.
The filling
In a small bowl combine 5 tablespoons of room-tempature butter with 1 cup of brown sugar and 2 tablespoons of cinnamon mix until it forms a paste.
