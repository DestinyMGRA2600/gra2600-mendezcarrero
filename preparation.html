How to make Cinnamon Rolls

Home   Dough and Filling   Prepare the Cinnamon Roll   How to bake

Roll out the Dough
After the dough has risen, take it out of the bowl and place it on a flat surface that has been lightly floured. Roll the dough out into a rectangle shape that is about 1/4 of an inch thick. Make sure the dough is evenly rolled out and not too thin or too thick. If the dough is sticking to the surface, sprinkle a little more flour on top of the dough and on the rolling pin. Once the dough is rolled out, it is ready for the filling to be added.
The filling
With a spatula, spread the filling evenly over the rolled out dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
Form the Roll
Take a pizza cuter or sharp knife and cut the dough into 12 equal pieces. Cut the dough vertically, after being cut roll each piece into a spiral shape.
